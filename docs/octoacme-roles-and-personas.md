# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support delivery by aligning staffing, technical execution, and team health with project goals. They help remove delivery risks when people, capacity, or technical trade-offs need management support.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm engineering capacity and role coverage for planned work
- Coach developers and help resolve delivery blockers
- Partner with PM and Product Manager on scope, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Escalate staffing, dependency, or technical execution risks early
- Support performance, team health, and sustainable delivery practices

### Goals
- Keep the engineering team staffed and focused on the highest-value work
- Improve predictability, quality, and team health
- Reduce delivery risk caused by capacity gaps or unclear ownership

### Typical Communication
- Weekly delivery alignment with PM and Product Manager
- Team health, staffing, and dependency discussions with developers
- Escalations and status updates for leadership or sponsors when needed

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: confirms team capacity, delivery risks, and escalation paths during planning and execution
- With Product Managers: aligns scope and sequencing to match capacity and technical constraints
- With Developers: provides coaching, clears blockers, and supports technical decision-making
- With QA/Testing: helps prioritize quality work and resolve resourcing gaps affecting test coverage
- With Stakeholders: joins escalation or milestone discussions when delivery risk requires engineering leadership

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape the user experience, workflows, and interface details needed to deliver usable solutions. They help the team translate business goals into designs that users can understand and adopt.

### Responsibilities
- Create user flows, wireframes, and interface designs
- Contribute user research insights and usability considerations
- Define interaction details and edge cases for delivery teams
- Partner with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and experience outcomes
- Support design QA before release

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and consistent user experiences
- Reduce rework caused by unclear workflows or usability gaps
- Ensure shipped solutions solve real user problems

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers and developers
- Requirement clarification during planning and backlog refinement
- Usability feedback and design QA notes during execution

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: aligns design milestones and reviews with the delivery plan
- With Product Managers: turns product goals into user flows, interface requirements, and acceptance details
- With Developers: hands off designs, clarifies edge cases, and reviews implementation fidelity
- With QA/Testing: shares expected behavior and design acceptance points for feature validation
- With Stakeholders: presents design options and incorporates business or customer feedback

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps / Platform Engineers enable reliable delivery environments, deployment automation, and operational readiness. They help teams release safely and consistently across environments.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment workflows
- Provision or support environments, secrets handling, and platform access
- Improve observability, reliability, and rollback readiness
- Partner with teams on release automation and operational controls
- Identify platform constraints and deployment risks early

### Goals
- Reduce release friction and environment-related delays
- Improve deployment reliability and recovery time
- Standardize delivery practices across teams

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and readiness reviews
- CI/CD issue triage with developers and QA
- Operational updates during releases or incidents

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: confirms environment readiness, deployment dependencies, and operational risk
- With Product Managers: advises on release constraints that affect sequencing or launch timing
- With Developers: supports build, deployment, observability, and infrastructure changes
- With QA/Testing: prepares test environments and helps stabilize automation or test data dependencies
- With Stakeholders: provides deployment status and operational readiness input for release decisions

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads help the team meet security, privacy, and regulatory expectations without losing sight of delivery goals. They focus on control points, risk treatment, and required approvals.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and compliance requirements during planning
- Review high-risk changes, controls, and exception requests
- Define required evidence for audits, approvals, or customer commitments
- Partner on incident readiness, access controls, and secure release practices
- Escalate material security or compliance risks

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk in delivered work
- Ensure required controls and evidence are present before release
- Help teams address security needs early rather than late

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews during planning and release readiness
- Security guidance and approval requests for higher-risk work
- Incident or exception communication when controls are at risk

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: identifies review gates, required approvals, and risk owners
- With Product Managers: clarifies policy or customer requirements that influence scope and acceptance criteria
- With Developers: reviews secure implementation needs, access controls, and remediation priorities
- With QA/Testing: confirms security test scope, validation evidence, and issue severity handling
- With Stakeholders: communicates material risks, required controls, and approval status when needed

---

## Customer Success / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Representatives bring customer context, support readiness, and post-launch feedback into the project lifecycle. They help teams prepare for adoption and respond to customer impact quickly.

### Responsibilities
- Share common customer pain points and support trends
- Review release impact on customer workflows and support materials
- Prepare launch communications, FAQs, or support playbooks
- Surface post-release issues, adoption risks, and training needs
- Help validate readiness for customer-facing changes

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption and reduce avoidable support volume
- Ensure support teams are ready for launches and incidents
- Feed customer feedback back into prioritization and improvement work

### Typical Communication
- Launch readiness coordination with PM, Product, and release owners
- Support enablement notes, FAQs, and customer-impact summaries
- Feedback loops after release on customer issues and adoption signals

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: aligns launch timing, readiness checkpoints, and communication plans
- With Product Managers: shares customer pain points and post-release feedback that inform priorities
- With Developers: relays reproducible customer issues or operational pain points
- With QA/Testing: helps identify customer-critical scenarios for validation or smoke testing
- With Stakeholders: communicates customer impact, rollout readiness, and support status

---

## Data Analysts / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data Analysts / Analytics Leads help teams define, instrument, and interpret the metrics used to measure project outcomes. They connect delivery work to evidence about customer behavior and business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define reporting needs and measurement plans for success metrics
- Validate event tracking, dashboards, and data quality assumptions
- Support experiments, baselines, and post-launch analysis
- Turn data into decision-ready insights for product and project reviews
- Highlight measurement gaps that could block outcome tracking

### Goals
- Ensure teams can measure whether delivered work is successful
- Improve decision quality with timely, trustworthy data
- Reduce ambiguity around impact and follow-up priorities

### Typical Communication
- Metric definition and instrumentation planning sessions
- Dashboard reviews and outcome readouts with product and project leads
- Analysis summaries after release or milestone checkpoints

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: aligns reporting checkpoints and owners for measurement deliverables
- With Product Managers: refines success metrics, hypotheses, and follow-up questions
- With Developers: coordinates instrumentation, event definitions, and data-quality fixes
- With QA/Testing: validates that analytics events and reporting hooks behave as expected
- With Stakeholders: shares outcome reporting and explains the data behind key decisions

---

## Technical Writers / Documentation Owners

### Role Summary
Technical Writers / Documentation Owners ensure project outputs are supported by clear internal and external documentation. They help teams keep process, support, and user-facing guidance accurate as work changes.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and draft required documentation updates
- Maintain release notes, user guidance, or internal process content
- Review documentation for accuracy, completeness, and audience fit
- Coordinate documentation handoffs before release
- Track documentation gaps discovered during execution or support

### Goals
- Reduce confusion caused by outdated or missing documentation
- Improve adoption, onboarding, and support readiness
- Make documentation updates part of the delivery definition of done

### Typical Communication
- Documentation requests and review cycles with product and engineering
- Release-note coordination during launch preparation
- Feedback loops with support and stakeholders on missing guidance

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: aligns documentation deliverables with milestones and release readiness
- With Product Managers: turns feature intent and acceptance criteria into clear user or internal guidance
- With Developers: validates technical details, setup steps, and known limitations
- With QA/Testing: uses test results and edge cases to improve documentation accuracy
- With Stakeholders: shares release notes, enablement content, or process updates for review

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers are an optional role used when release coordination is distinct from the Project Manager. They coordinate release readiness, deployment sequencing, and go/no-go checkpoints across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Own release calendars, cutover checklists, and readiness reviews
- Confirm required approvals, testing, and rollback plans are complete
- Coordinate deployment timing across engineering, QA, support, and stakeholders
- Track release-specific risks, dependencies, and communications
- Lead go/no-go and post-release verification follow-through

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, low-risk releases
- Reduce confusion around who owns launch readiness decisions
- Improve consistency of release evidence and communications

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go meetings
- Deployment timelines and checklists shared with delivery teams
- Launch announcements and post-release status updates

### Key Interactions & Handoffs
- With Project Managers: takes over release coordination when launch complexity needs a distinct owner
- With Product Managers: confirms launch scope, timing, and customer-impact communications
- With Developers: coordinates release candidates, deployment sequencing, and rollback readiness
- With QA/Testing: confirms test sign-off, known issues, and post-deploy verification coverage
- With Stakeholders: communicates readiness status, launch decisions, and post-release outcomes

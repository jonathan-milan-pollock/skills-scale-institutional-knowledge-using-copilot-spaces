# OctoAcme — Handoff & Operational Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist before production release and at handoff to support/operations.

## Service Readiness
- [ ] Release scope and go/no-go decision are recorded in the Decision Log
- [ ] Runbook is updated with deploy, rollback, and troubleshooting steps
- [ ] Monitoring dashboards and alerts are reviewed and active
- [ ] Known risks and mitigations are documented in the risk register
- [ ] On-call coverage and escalation path are confirmed

## Quality & Deployment Readiness
- [ ] CI, security checks, and smoke tests passed
- [ ] Data migration/backfill steps validated (if applicable)
- [ ] Rollback criteria and owner confirmed
- [ ] Release notes drafted and reviewed

## Handoff Readiness
- [ ] Support team briefed on changes, known issues, and customer impact
- [ ] Ownership after launch is explicit (engineering + product + support)
- [ ] Post-release verification plan and timeline are defined
- [ ] Follow-up review date is scheduled

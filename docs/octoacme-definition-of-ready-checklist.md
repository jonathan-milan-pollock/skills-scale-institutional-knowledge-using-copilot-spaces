# OctoAcme — Definition of Ready Checklist

Use this checklist before pulling work into an iteration.

## Backlog Item Readiness
- [ ] Problem statement is clear and customer value is defined
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable and unambiguous
- [ ] Dependencies are identified (teams, systems, vendors)
- [ ] UX/content/API requirements are linked (if applicable)
- [ ] Security/privacy considerations are documented
- [ ] Rollout constraints are known (flags, migration, sequencing)
- [ ] Effort estimate exists and team capacity is validated
- [ ] Owner is assigned and accountable in the RACI matrix

## Team Readiness
- [ ] Open decisions are logged with owners and due dates
- [ ] QA approach is defined (unit/integration/e2e/manual as needed)
- [ ] Success metric or outcome signal is identified
- [ ] Stakeholders aligned on priority and timing
